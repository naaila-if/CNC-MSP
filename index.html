Forward Pricing

Three models, same workload

Growth is good, and we want a structure that works for both of us as it continues. These options are built to keep hours and cost predictable on both sides going forward.

Applied to actual volume: 65 users, ~43 freelancer events/mo, ~2.8 devices/mo.

One Rate, Not Two

Normally we bill full-time and freelance users under separate rate structures, since freelancer onboarding and offboarding usually carries its own per-event cost. Under Model 2, freelancers are covered under the same flat per-user rate as full-time staff, no separate freelancer pricing, no per-event charges. Given CNC's freelancer volume, that keeps pricing simpler and more predictable.

Model 1 & Model 2 · All You Can Eat

What's included in both flat models

Both Model 1 and Model 2 cover the day-to-day Techcierge service scope below. Full-time onboarding and offboarding are included in both models. Freelancer onboarding and offboarding are included in Model 2; under Model 1, those freelancer events remain billed separately at $50 each.

Model 1 caveats (not present in Model 2)

• Freelancer onboard and offboard events are billed separately at $50 each, not covered by the flat rate

• Laptop setup is billed separately at $150 per device, including lifetime maintenance, rather than the $75 onboarding fee under Model 2 (which also includes free maintenance and support for the life of the device after that one-time charge)

Everything else below is included under both models at no additional charge.

Project Work & Other Exclusions

Project scoping is included. Techcierge will help define requirements, assess the environment, recommend an approach, and prepare scope/estimates for upcoming work. The execution of project work itself — including migrations, rollouts, network redesign, bulk deployments of 5+ devices, policy or compliance documentation, and on-site implementation — is not included in the flat monthly rate and is billed separately once approved. Emergency or weekend support outside standard hours is also billed separately. Computer purchasing and procurement coordination are included; the actual cost of hardware, software, licenses, shipping, taxes, and other third-party purchases is paid separately by CNC. Device setup/onboarding is not included in the flat monthly rate and is billed at the applicable per-device setup fee.
